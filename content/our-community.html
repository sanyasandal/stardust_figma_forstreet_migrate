Our Community

Fort Street has a strong and supportive community of both past and present students and families.

Fort Street Alumni

Fort Street Alumni Community members of work with the school to archive and display important records, memorabilia and documents in our museum from all three schools throughout history.

The Parents and Citizens Association (P&C)

The P&C is highly active and contributes substantially to the life of Fort Street High School. It provides a forum which enables parents to get involved in the organisation and direction of the school.

east

The Fort Street Foundation

The Fort Street Foundation a registered charity and supports the school financially for initiatives including the development of buildings and facilities.

east

The Fortians Union

The Fortian Union began in 1894 and promotes social fellowship, reunions, assists the school and preserves its traditions.
