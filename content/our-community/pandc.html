Parents and Citizens Association (P&C)

The Parents and Citizens Association (P&C) is highly active and contributes substantially to the life of Fort Street High School. It provides a forum which enables parents to get involved in the organisation and direction of the school. The P&C initiates and supports programs to enhance student learning, manages the uniform shop and assists with running and fundraising for the Instrumental Music Program (IMP).

Fort Street High School P&C Association is a group of parents and others who volunteer their time to help our school provide the best possible learning environment for all our children.

Their aims are to:

  • raise money for the school
  • foster and improve communications between parents and the school
  • enhance a sense of school community
  • assist the school by establishing subcommittees which support the development of programs within the school (e.g. Instrumental Music Program, Uniform Shop, Sport).

Parents are encouraged to be involved in whatever way suits their skills, interests and availability. The P&C usually meets in the school library at 7:30pm on the second Wednesday of each month during term. All parents are most welcome to attend and there are many opportunities to get involved.

Governance

The P&C is a body corporate under the Parents & Citizens’ Association Incorporation Act 1976, the constitution can be viewed here.

Fundraising

One of the roles of the P&C is fundraising, which is contributed to the school, to provide better facilities than government funding covers.

The funds that the P&C can make available to the school come from P&C memberships, uniform shop sales and the proceeds from events such as Fort Fest, IMP concerts and Senior Drama nights.

In 2019 the P&C gave the school more than $200,000 to provide sporting facilities, classroom technology, faculty assets and numerous other items.

Grants

The P&C is also active in applying for local, state and federal government grants. Funds received from these grants enable the school to build better facilities for our students and run projects to improve educational outcomes.

In 2020 the P&C won grants to refurbish the library foyer and establish the Kitchen Garden and composting program.

If you have information about upcoming grants, experience with or an interest in applying for grants, please contact the P&C. (opens in new window)

Community events

Community Events run by the P&C aim to build community awareness within the school, give parents, students and other supporters an opportunity to meet informally and to contribute to the school and to raise funds.

Meetings

The P&C holds meetings usually at 7:30pm in the school library on the second Wednesday of each month during school terms. All meetings are hybrid and so may also be joined online via the link sent out via email.

These meetings provide an opportunity to learn more about what is happening in the school, how it operates, to ask questions and to meet other parents.

The Principal usually gives an informative report on the recent happenings within the school.

The school emails details of upcoming meetings directly to parents and carers prior to the date. This email will include a copy of the upcoming agenda.
